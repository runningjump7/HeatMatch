# HeatMatch Installer Partner Program

## Welcome to HeatMatch.nz

HeatMatch connects homeowners searching for heat pump solutions with qualified local installers. We're building New Zealand's most trusted heat pump marketplace.

---

## What You Get

### 6-Month Free Pilot
- **Exclusive leads** in your territory (one lead = one installer)
- **Qualified homeowners** actively seeking quotes
- **No signup fees** or monthly costs during pilot
- **No commitment** - cancel anytime, no questions asked

### Lead Quality
Every lead includes:
- Homeowner name, phone, email
- Property details (size, type, location)
- Heat pump requirements (rooms, budget, timeline)
- Pre-qualified intent (serious buyers only)

### After Pilot
If the partnership works for both of us:
- Monthly subscription (tiered options available)
- Details discussed at 6-month review
- Pricing based on lead volume and territory size

---

## What We Need From You

### Response Time
- Contact leads within **24 hours** (ideally same day)
- Faster response = higher conversion rates

### Feedback Loop
Help us improve lead quality:
- Rate each lead (1-5 stars)
- Quick note: "Great fit" or "Wrong budget/timeline"
- Takes 30 seconds per lead

### Job Updates
Keep us in the loop:
- **Quoted** - you've provided a quote
- **Won** - customer accepted, job booked
- **Lost** - didn't proceed (optional: why?)
- **Completed** - job finished

*Why this matters:* We use this data to improve matching and qualify leads better.

### 3. You Make Contact

### 5. We Learn & Improve
Your feedback helps us send better-matched leads.

## FAQs

**Q: What if a lead is poor quality?**  
A: Tell us immediately. We'll stop sending similar leads and may offer a replacement.

**Q: Can I pause leads during busy periods?**  
A: Yes! Toggle your availability on/off anytime in the dashboard.

**Q: Do I compete with other installers?**  
A: No. Each lead goes to ONE installer in your area. No bidding wars.

**Q: What happens after 6 months?**  
A: We'll review together. If it's working, we transition to a monthly subscription. If not, part ways as friends.

**Q: Who owns the customer relationship?**  
A: You do. Once we send the lead, they're your customer.
