## Phone Script (When You Call After Email)

**Opening:**
"Hi [Name], it's Alex from HeatMatch. I emailed you earlier this week about our North Shore installer pilot program - did you get a chance to look at it?"

**If YES:**
"Great! Any questions I can answer? ... [Discuss] ... If you're keen, I just need about 10 minutes to get your details and we can start sending leads your way."

**If NO / DON'T RECALL:**
"No worries - quick 30-second pitch: We're connecting North Shore homeowners with local installers. Free leads for 6 months, exclusive territory, no competing with other installers. We're only taking 2-3 for the pilot. Sound interesting?"

**Objection Handling:**
- **"Already busy"** → "Perfect - this is for when things slow down or to smooth out seasonal dips. Lock in your spot now before we fill up."
- **"What's the catch?"** → "Transparent: free for 6 months to prove it works. Then a monthly subscription. If it doesn't work out, we part ways - no hard feelings."
- **"Bad experience with lead sites"** → "That's exactly why we're different. One lead, one installer. You're not competing on price with 5 others."

**Close:**
"Great! Let me grab your details real quick and I'll have you set up this week..."

## Tips for Sending

1. **Personalize the opening:** Mention their company name or something specific (e.g., "I saw your 5-star reviews on Google")
2. **Send Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11am:** Best open rates for trades
3. **Attach the PDF:** "Installer-Onboarding.pdf"
4. **Follow up:** If no response after 2-3 days, send follow-up or call
5. **Track responses:** Use a simple spreadsheet to track who you emailed, when, and response status
