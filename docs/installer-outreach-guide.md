## 1. How to Find North Shore Installers

### Google Maps (Best Source)
1. Search: **"heat pump installer north shore auckland"**
2. Filter by ratings: 4+ stars minimum
3. Click through to website, note contact details
4. Check they service residential (not just commercial)

### Trade Associations
- **EECA (Energy Efficiency & Conservation Authority):** https://genless.govt.nz/
- **Master Electricians NZ:** https://www.masterelectricians.org.nz/
- Many heat pump installers are electricians with HVAC certs

### Review Sites
- **Nocowboys.co.nz** - search heat pump installers
- **Google Reviews** - sort by highest rated
- **Facebook Reviews/Pages** - local trade groups

### Competitor Research
- Check who advertises on **heatpumps.co.nz**
- Look at **Trade Me Services** ads
- Check **Yellow Pages** (yes, still relevant for trades)

### Referral Network
- Ask anyone you know who's had a heat pump installed
- Post in local Facebook groups (tactfully): "Who did your heat pump?"
- LinkedIn search: "heat pump installer auckland"

## 2. Vetting Criteria (Before You Email)

✅ **Must Have:**
- Licensed and insured
- 3+ years experience (check website/About page)
- Good online reviews (4+ stars, 10+ reviews)
- Services residential (not just commercial)
- Active business (recent Google reviews, updated website)

❌ **Red Flags - Avoid:**
- Multiple bad reviews mentioning poor communication
- No licensing/insurance info on website
- Website hasn't been updated in 3+ years
- No online presence at all (no website, no reviews)
- Aggressive sales tactics in reviews
- Previously went by different business names (check reviews)

---

## 3. Research Before Emailing

Spend **2 minutes per installer** gathering:
- **Contact name** (owner/manager if possible)
- **Company name** (spell it correctly!)
- **Email address** (check website contact page)
- **Phone number** (backup for follow-up)
- **One specific detail:** Recent review, specialty, award, years in business

**Pro tip:** Use this detail in your email opening to show you've done homework:
> "Hi John, I came across ABC Heat Pumps and noticed your 5-star reviews..."

---

## 4. Outreach Approach

### The Process
1. **Email first** with PDF attached (use template)
2. **Wait 2-3 days** for response
3. **Follow-up email** if no response
4. **Call 1-2 days later** if still no response
5. **Move on** after 3 touchpoints (don't be pushy)

### Best Times to Send
- **Tuesday - Thursday**
- **9-11am** (after morning site visits, before lunch)
- **Avoid Mondays** (catching up from weekend)
- **Avoid Fridays PM** (mentally checked out)

### Email Personalization Checklist
- [ ] Use their first name (not "Dear Sir/Madam")
- [ ] Spell company name correctly
- [ ] Include one specific detail about their business
- [ ] Attach PDF onboarding doc
- [ ] Clear subject line with urgency ("2 spots left")

## 5. Objection Handling

### "I'm already too busy"
**Response:** "That's great to hear business is strong! This is really for when things slow down seasonally, or to smooth out demand. Lock in your North Shore territory now - we're only taking 2-3 installers and spots are filling up."

**Why it works:** Frames it as strategic planning, not desperate need. FOMO on territory.

---

### "What's the catch?"
**Response:** "No catch - completely transparent. Free for 6 months so you can test the lead quality. After that, simple monthly subscription. If it doesn't work out, we part ways as friends. You've got nothing to lose."

**Why it works:** Addresses trust concern directly. Low risk, high transparency.

---

### "I've tried lead-gen sites before - they're rubbish"
**Response:** "I hear you - most sites send your lead to 5 other installers, so you're competing on price in a race to the bottom. We're different: one lead goes to ONE installer. Exclusive territory. No bidding wars."

**Why it works:** Acknowledges their pain, shows you understand the problem, positions as solution.

---

### "How many leads will I get?"
**Response:** "Honest answer: we're just launching, so I can't guarantee volume yet. That's why it's free for 6 months - you help us prove the model works. Based on early interest, I'm expecting 2-5 leads per week to start, growing from there."

**Why it works:** Honesty builds trust. Sets realistic expectations. Shows it's a partnership.

---

### "Can I see an example lead?"
**Response:** "Not yet - we're pre-launch. But each lead includes: homeowner name, contact details, property type, rooms needing heat pumps, budget range, and timeline. All pre-qualified through our website form."

**Why it works:** Gives concrete detail without overpromising. Shows you've thought it through.

---

### "What if the leads are poor quality?"
**Response:** "That's exactly why I need your feedback. If a lead is a time-waster, tell me immediately and I'll adjust our screening. You're helping build this, so your input directly improves the quality."

**Why it works:** Makes them feel like a partner, not a customer. Shows responsiveness.

---

### "I need to think about it"
**Response:** "Absolutely, take your time. Just so you know, we're limiting to 2-3 North Shore installers, and I've already had [X] expressions of interest. If you want to lock in your spot, just reply 'Interested' and we'll hold it for you while you decide."

**Why it works:** Soft close with urgency. Removes friction (just say "interested").

---

### "Can I pause during busy times?"
**Response:** "Yes! You can toggle your availability on/off anytime. If you're slammed with work, just let me know and we'll pause leads until you're ready again."

**Why it works:** Removes a major concern about being overwhelmed.

## 7. Phone Follow-Up Script

**When:** 2-3 days after follow-up email with no response

**Opening:**
> "Hi [Name], it's Alex from HeatMatch. I sent you an email last week about our North Shore installer pilot - did you get a chance to look at it?"

**If YES, interested:**
> "Awesome! What questions can I answer?... [Listen]... Great, if you're keen, I just need 10 minutes to collect your details and you'll start getting leads."

**If YES, not sure:**
> "No worries - what's holding you back?... [Listen, address objection]... Tell you what, why don't we start with just one lead? Zero commitment, see how it goes."

**If NO / don't recall:**
> "No problem - super quick pitch: We connect North Shore homeowners with local installers. Free leads for 6 months, you're the only installer we send each lead to. We're taking just 2-3 for the pilot. Worth a 10-minute chat?"

**If still hesitant:**
> "Fair enough - I'll send you the one-pager again. If you change your mind, spots are filling up fast, so don't wait too long. Sound good?"

**Close:**
> "Thanks for your time, [Name]. I'll check back in a couple of weeks when we've got more traction."

## 8. Red Flags During Conversation

Walk away if they:
- ❌ Ask for upfront payment
- ❌ Want you to guarantee X leads per week (you can't, not yet)
- ❌ Seem disorganized or hard to reach (won't respond to customer leads)
- ❌ Bad-mouth previous customers (red flag for service quality)
- ❌ Refuse to provide licensing/insurance proof
- ❌ Want exclusive rights to ALL of Auckland (unrealistic)
- ❌ Won't commit to 24-hour response times

**Remember:** You're choosing partners, not just collecting anyone.

## 13. Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Generic emails:** "Dear Sir/Madam" gets ignored  
✅ **Do this:** "Hi John, saw your 5-star reviews..."

❌ **Too pushy:** Calling 5 times in 2 days  
✅ **Do this:** Email → follow-up email → one call → move on

❌ **Overpromising:** "You'll get 20 leads per week!"  
✅ **Do this:** "Expecting 2-5/week to start, growing from there"

❌ **No follow-up:** Sending one email then giving up  
✅ **Do this:** 3 touchpoints (email, follow-up email, call)

❌ **Desperation:** "Please, I really need installers!"  
✅ **Do this:** "Limited to 2-3 spots, filling up fast"

❌ **Ignoring red flags:** Taking anyone who says yes  
✅ **Do this:** Vet properly - protect your brand

## 14. Scripts for Difficult Situations

### They Ask: "Why should I trust you?"
"Fair question. Here's what I can offer: try it free for 6 months. If the leads are rubbish, you've lost nothing but a bit of time. If they're good, you've gained a consistent lead source. All I'm asking is a chance to prove it works."

### They Ask: "How much will this cost after 6 months?"
"We're looking at $150-$400/month depending on lead volume and territory size. But honestly, let's see if it works first. If you're not getting value after 6 months, we won't charge you. Fair?"

### They Want Guaranteed Lead Volume
"I can't guarantee volume yet - we're just launching. What I can guarantee is that you'll be the only installer we send North Shore leads to. Quality over quantity. If we're only sending 1-2 leads/week, we won't charge you a subscription."

### They Want to Think About It (and you sense they're not interested)
"No worries at all. Can I ask - is there something specific holding you back, or is it just not the right time? ... [Listen] ... Fair enough. I'll keep you on my list for when we expand to wider Auckland later this year."

## 16. If You Get Stuck

### Low Response Rate (<20%)
- Check: Are subject lines compelling?
- Check: Are you personalizing emails?
- Check: Is PDF attached?
- Test: Try different email template (V2 or V3)
- Test: Call directly instead of emailing first

### Interested But Won't Commit
- Offer: "Let's start with just ONE lead - zero commitment"
- Reduce friction: Make onboarding super easy (5 min phone call)
- Add urgency: "Other installers expressing interest..."

### Too Many Rejections
- Ask: "What would make this more appealing?"
- Listen: They might reveal objections you haven't considered
- Pivot: Adjust offer based on feedback

### Analysis Paralysis (You Keep Researching, Not Sending)
- **Just send 5 emails.** Imperfect action > perfect planning.
- Worst case: they don't reply. So what?
- Best case: you get 2 partners by Friday.
